Questions

Is this allowed by Instagram?

Yes. You download your own data with Instagram's official export tool, then choose to open it here. SaveSort never logs into your account, scrapes Instagram or touches anyone else's data.

How do I get my saves file?
  1. Open Instagram, then Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions, then Download your information. Some accounts call it Export your information.
  3. Choose your Instagram account, Some of your information, and tick Saved.
  4. Pick format HTML and create the file.
  5. When Instagram emails you, download the zip and drop it straight in. No need to unzip it.

There's a longer guide with every screen on the blog.

HTML or JSON?

Choose HTML. It includes each post's caption, which is what SaveSort reads to sort and flag your saves. JSON only includes links, so everything lands in "Needs the video".

Does it watch the videos?

No. The free sort reads each save's caption, and Pro breakdowns are written by AI from the caption too. Reels with almost no caption are labelled "Needs the video" so you know which ones to watch yourself.

How accurate is the free sort?

It sorts by the words in each caption, so it's quick but not perfect. A gym reel that mentions "business" might land in the wrong topic. A Pro breakdown reads the whole caption and can correct the label, but it's AI-written, so check prices and claims before you spend money.

Does it work for TikTok or YouTube saves?

Not yet. Today SaveSort reads Instagram's saved-posts export only.

Can I cancel Pro?

Any time. You keep Pro until the end of the week you've paid for, and you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a charge. See refunds and cancellations.

Is SaveSort part of Instagram or Meta?

No. SaveSort is independent and isn't affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
