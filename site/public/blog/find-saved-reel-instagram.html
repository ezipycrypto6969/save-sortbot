How to find a saved reel on Instagram

Where Instagram keeps your saved reels, how to show only reels, why there's no search, and how to search every saved caption at once.

By the SaveSort team. 15 November 2026, 4 min read.

Your saved reels are in your profile menu under Saved, then All posts, where you can switch to show only reels. There's no search inside Saved, so if you have hundreds, the fastest way to find one is to search the captions in Instagram's data export instead.

How do I see my saved reels in the app?

  1. Open Instagram and tap your profile picture in the bottom right.
  2. Tap the menu (three lines) in the top right.
  3. Tap Saved. It opens on All posts, with any collections you've made next to it.
  4. Tap the reels icon at the top of the grid to show only reels.
  5. Scroll. Your newest saves are at the top.

On a computer at instagram.com, open the More menu (three lines) at the bottom left, choose Saved, then All posts.

Can I search my saved reels?

Not inside Instagram. Saved lets you pick a collection and a post type, and that's it: you can't search by caption, creator or topic. If you remember roughly when you saved something, scrolling works. If you saved it months ago, it can take a long time.

How do I search every saved caption at once?

Instagram will send you a file of everything you've saved, with the link and caption of each post. Because it's an ordinary web page, your browser's find tool can search it.

  1. Request your saved posts from Instagram: tick only Saved, choose HTML. Instagram emails you a zip, usually within a few hours.
  2. Unzip it and open saved_posts.html (it's in the "saved" folder) in your browser.
  3. Use Find in page (Ctrl+F, or Cmd+F on a Mac; on a phone it's in the browser's menu) and type a word you remember: "pasta", "glutes", "budget", the name of a tool.
  4. Tap the link under the matching caption to open the reel on Instagram.

This only finds words that are in the caption. If the reel's caption was just a few emojis, you'll need another clue, like roughly when you saved it.

What if you don't remember any words?

Sometimes you only remember the kind of thing it was: a recipe, a workout, an AI tool. That's where sorting by topic helps.

Drop the same zip into SaveSort without unzipping it. It reads the saved-posts file in your browser, groups every save by topic and gives each one a label: Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked. Pick a topic and you're looking at a few dozen reels instead of the whole pile, each with a link back to Instagram. The file is never uploaded.

Honest limits: SaveSort has no search box either; it narrows things down by topic and label. It sorts by caption, so a reel with no caption can land in the wrong group. Our sample report had 156 saves, and 65 of them were labelled Watch it because the caption said too little to judge without watching.

Why did a saved reel disappear?

A save is a bookmark to someone else's post, not a copy. If the creator deletes the reel, deactivates their account or makes it private, it drops out of your Saved, and Instagram doesn't tell you. There's no way to get it back from Instagram's side.

If a reel really matters, write down the key steps or the name of the tool when you save it. A recipe you've copied into your notes can't vanish.

Didn't save it?

If you liked the reel instead of saving it, look in the menu under Your activity, then Likes, where you can filter to reels. Some versions of the app also show a Watch history for reels in Your activity. Menus move around between updates, so if yours looks different, check under Your activity first.
