How to download your Instagram saved posts

The exact steps to get a file of every post and reel you've saved on Instagram, which format to choose, and what's inside the zip.

By the SaveSort team. 11 November 2026, 4 min read.

Instagram has an official tool that sends you a copy of your own data, including everything in your Saved tab. It's free and you can use it as often as you like. You'll need your Instagram password to request the file.

The steps

  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile. Tap the menu in the top right to open Settings.
  2. Tap Accounts Center. Meta is renaming this for some people, so yours may say Meta Account.
  3. Tap Your information and permissions.
  4. Tap Download your information. Some accounts show Export your information instead. It's the same tool.
  5. Start a new download (or export) and choose your Instagram account.
  6. Choose Some of your information, then tick only Saved. It's under "Your Instagram activity".
  7. Choose to download to your device. Set the date range to All time and the format to HTML.
  8. Create the file. Instagram emails you a link when it's ready.

Shortcut: this link opens the right screen directly if you're logged in: accountscenter.instagram.com.

How long does it take?

With only Saved ticked, the file is small and the email usually arrives within a few hours. Bigger requests can take longer. Ticking only Saved is the fastest option and also the most private, because nothing else about your account is in the file.

HTML or JSON?

Instagram offers two formats. HTML opens as a normal page in your browser. JSON is raw data meant for other programs.

For your saves, choose HTML. In the exports we've tested, the HTML version includes each post's caption, while the JSON version only lists links. Without captions there's nothing to sort by.

What's inside the zip

The download is a zip file. Your saves are in one file inside it:

your_instagram_activity/saved/saved_posts.html

Open it in a browser and you'll see each saved post with its link and, usually, its caption. They're listed in the order you saved them, with no topics or search.

What to do with it

  • Keep it as a backup. If you ever delete your account, your saves go with it. The file is your copy.
  • Sort it. Drop the zip into SaveSort without unzipping it. It opens only saved_posts.html, in your browser, and groups every save by niche and type, with a label: Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked. The file is never uploaded.

Saved more reels since? Request a new file and drop it in again. See also: three ways to organise your saved posts.
