Apps that organise Instagram saved posts, compared

Instagram collections, SaveSort, Ordo, Vestron, Instavault and a Chrome extension: how each one organises your saved posts, and which suits whom.

By the SaveSort team. 13 November 2026, 5 min read.

Short answer: There are two kinds of tool. Share-to-app organisers (Ordo, Vestron, Instavault and others) sort posts you send them from now on. Export-based tools like SaveSort sort everything you've already saved in one go, from Instagram's own data download, with no login. If your problem is a backlog of hundreds of old saves, start with an export-based tool; if you want new saves filed automatically, try a share-to-app organiser.

We make SaveSort, so read this knowing that. We've tried to describe every option fairly, including when another one is the better choice. Details about other apps come from their own listings as of 13 November 2026, not from our testing, so check their sites for anything that's changed.

Two different jobs

  • Sorting what you've already saved. Most people's problem is hundreds of old saves. Tools that read Instagram's data export can sort all of them at once.
  • Filing new saves as you go. Share-to-app organisers sort posts you send them from now on. They usually don't touch your existing Saved tab.

Where SaveSort fits, and where it doesn't

  • Good for: a big backlog you want sorted today, without installing anything or handing over your login. It also flags hype ("Skip it") and reels that hide the steps behind "comment GUIDE" ("Locked").
  • Not for: search across your saves, sorting new saves automatically, or TikTok and YouTube. For those, a share-to-app organiser is the better fit.

Questions

What is the best app to organise Instagram saved posts?

It depends on your problem. For a big backlog of saves you already have, an export-based tool like SaveSort sorts them all at once. For filing new saves as you go, a share-to-app organiser like Ordo (iPhone) or Vestron (Android) fits better.

Is there a free way to organise Instagram saves?

Yes. Instagram's own collections are free, and SaveSort's sorter is free with no limit. Several share-to-app organisers also list a free version.

Do these apps need my Instagram password?

SaveSort doesn't: you download your own file from Instagram. Share-to-app organisers work through Instagram's share button or by messaging an account. Check each app's privacy policy for exactly what it stores.
