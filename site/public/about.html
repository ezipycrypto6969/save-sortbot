It started with one Saved tab.

SaveSort is a one-person project from Perth, Western Australia. It exists because saving a reel feels like doing something, and it isn't.

In October 2026 our founder exported his own Instagram saves. There were 156 of them: AI tools, side hustles, motivation clips, tax news, and a lot of "comment GUIDE and I'll send it to you".

Sorted, the pile looked very different. That report is the sample you can load on the home page, and these are its real numbers:

156reels saved
30worth doing
65needed the video to make sense
10hype or grey areas

Another 22 were gated guides: the reel tells you to comment a word to get the actual steps.

What we believe

  • Your file stays with you. The free sort runs in your browser. We never ask for your Instagram login, and your export is never uploaded.
  • Only true numbers. Every figure on this site and in our videos comes from a real report. No invented users, reviews or results.
  • We call out hype. We don't sell it. No get-rich-quick promises. If a reel's claim looks too good, SaveSort says so.
  • Small price, no lock-in. Sorting is free. Pro is A$1.99 a week, and you can cancel any time.

Who is Sorty?

Sorty is the blue bookmark you may have seen in our videos. Sorty is our own character, drawn and animated in code, and has opinions about how many reels you've saved.

How SaveSort is run

SaveSort is run by a sole trader in Perth. The sorter is a single web page, Pro breakdowns are written with Anthropic's Claude, and payments go through Stripe. Much of the day-to-day work, including our videos, is automated so the price can stay low.

SaveSort isn't affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
