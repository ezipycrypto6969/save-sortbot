Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Short version: the free sorter runs in your browser. Your Instagram file is read on your device and is never uploaded, stored or shared by SaveSort.

The free sorter

The Instagram file you choose is read on your device to build your report. It is never uploaded, stored or shared by SaveSort. We don't use cookies or trackers to sell or share your data.

To save you choosing the file again, your last sorted list (links and captions only) is kept in your own browser on this device. Clear it any time with "Clear it from this device" above your report.

SaveSort Pro

Payments and subscriptions are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. We keep your Stripe subscription ID inside your Pro code so we can check it's active.

When you tap "Break it down" on a save, only that save's caption is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude API to write the breakdown. It isn't stored by us, and your file and other saves never leave your device.

Our own social accounts

SaveSort's own social accounts are run by a posting tool that uploads SaveSort's videos to SaveSort's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts. Where it uses Google sign-in (YouTube Data API), it only uploads videos to the SaveSort channel. It doesn't access, store or share any other person's Google or YouTube data.

SaveSort's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can review or remove an app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information (such as IP address) to deliver pages and protect the site. The site's fonts are served from savesortai.com, not from a third party.
