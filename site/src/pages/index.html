Questions

How do I sort all my saved Instagram reels at once?

Download your saves with Instagram's own export (Accounts Center, Download your information, tick Saved, format HTML), then drop the zip into SaveSort. It sorts every save by niche, type and label in your browser, in one go, without uploading the file. Step-by-step guide.

Is this allowed by Instagram?

Yes. You download your own data with Instagram's official export tool, then choose to open it here. SaveSort never logs into your account, scrapes Instagram or touches anyone else's data.

How do I get my saves file?
  1. Open Instagram, then Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions, then Download your information. Some accounts call it Export your information.
  3. Choose your Instagram account, Some of your information, and tick Saved.
  4. Pick date range All time and format HTML, then create the file.
  5. When Instagram emails you, download the zip and drop it straight in. No need to unzip it.

There's a longer guide with every screen on the blog.

HTML or JSON?

Choose HTML. It includes each post's caption, which is what SaveSort reads to sort and flag your saves. JSON only includes links, so everything lands in "Watch it".

Does it watch the videos?

No. The free sort reads each save's caption, and Pro breakdowns are written by AI from the caption too. Reels with almost no caption are labelled "Watch it" so you know which ones to watch yourself.

How accurate is the free sort?

It sorts by the words in each caption, so it's quick but not perfect. A gym reel that mentions "business" a lot might land in the wrong niche, and a joke can look like a tip. A Pro breakdown reads the whole caption and can correct the label, but it's AI-written, so check prices and claims before you spend money.

Does it work for TikTok or YouTube saves?

Not yet. Today SaveSort reads Instagram's saved-posts export only.

Can I cancel Pro?

Any time. You keep Pro until the end of the week you've paid for, and you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a charge. See refunds and cancellations.

Is SaveSort part of Instagram or Meta?

No. SaveSort is independent and isn't affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
