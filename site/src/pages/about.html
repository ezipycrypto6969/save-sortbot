Saving a reel feels like doing something. It isn't.

Your Saved tab is a to-do list nobody reads. SaveSort reads it for you, in seconds, and hands back the short list of what's worth your time.

The problem

  • You save a reel to "do it later". Later never comes.
  • Instagram shows your saves as one long grid, in the order you saved them. It doesn't sort them by niche or type.
  • In our sample report, four in ten captions said almost nothing, and one in seven hid the steps behind "comment GUIDE".
  • Hype and real advice look exactly the same in a grid of thumbnails.

What SaveSort does about it

  • Reads every save from the file Instagram gives you. One file, sorted in seconds.
  • Groups your saves by niche and by type (how-tos, tools, news, memes), so you can work through one pile at a time.
  • Labels each save: Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked.
  • Does it all in your browser. Your file is never uploaded.

Our sample report

Our sample report is one real Saved tab, sorted by SaveSort in October 2026: 156 reels about AI tools, side hustles, motivation and tax, plus a lot of "comment GUIDE and I'll send it to you".

Fewer than one in five were worth doing. A pile of 156 became a list of 30. The rest weren't lost: they stayed in the report, sorted by niche and type, with a label wherever the caption gave a reason.

Those are the real numbers from that report, out of 156 saves. Locked can overlap the other labels. You can load 40 of its captions on the home page to see how a report works.
