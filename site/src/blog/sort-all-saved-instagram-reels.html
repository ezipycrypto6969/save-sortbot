Saving a reel takes one tap. Sorting a few hundred of them by hand takes an evening, so most people never do it. The good news: you don't have to go through them one by one.

Why Instagram can't do it for you

Everything you save lands in one list, newest first. Instagram's only organising tool is collections: folders you create and fill yourself, one post at a time. That's fine for new saves, but it doesn't help with a backlog of hundreds.

Sort the whole backlog in one go

The trick is to get your saves out of the app as a file, then sort the file.

  1. Ask Instagram for your saves. Settings, Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your information (some accounts say Export your information). Choose Some of your information, tick only Saved, date range All time, format HTML. Every screen, step by step.
  2. Wait for the email. With only Saved ticked, the file is small and usually arrives within a few hours.
  3. Drop the zip into SaveSort. No unzipping. It opens only the saved-posts file inside, on your device, and nothing is uploaded.
  4. Work through one pile at a time. Every save is grouped by niche (AI, fitness, food, money and about 20 more) and by type (how-tos, tools and apps, news, explainers, motivation, brainrot and memes). Each also gets a label: Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked.

In our own sample report, 156 saved reels became a list of 30 worth doing. The rest weren't deleted, just sorted out of the way.

What it can't do: SaveSort doesn't change anything inside Instagram or make collections for you. It reads captions, so a reel with almost no caption is marked "Not enough caption" rather than guessed, and a joke written like a tip can land in the wrong pile.

Keep it sorted afterwards

  • Save into a collection as you go. Press and hold the bookmark icon to pick a collection when you save.
  • Re-sort every month or two. Download a fresh file and drop it in again. It takes a minute.
  • Unsave what you'll never do. If a save is marked Skip it or you can't remember why you saved it, let it go.
