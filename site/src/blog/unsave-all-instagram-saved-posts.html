Instagram has no single "Unsave all" button. In the app you can select several saved posts at once and unsave them together, which is the fastest built-in way; on instagram.com you unsave one post at a time. Before you clear anything, download a copy, because an unsaved post is gone from your Saved list.

How do I unsave several posts at once in the app?

  1. Open Instagram, tap your profile picture, then the menu (three lines) in the top right.
  2. Tap Saved, then All posts.
  3. Tap the three dots in the top right and choose Select.
  4. Tap each post you want gone. A tick appears on each one.
  5. Tap Unsave (some versions say Remove from saved) and confirm.

Guides written at different times disagree on whether every phone shows the Select option, and there's no "select all". If you don't see it, update the app. On a big pile you'll be doing this a screenful at a time.

How do I unsave one post?

Open the post from your Saved list and tap the bookmark icon under it so it's no longer filled in. On a computer at instagram.com, open More, then Saved, open the post and click the same bookmark icon. The website doesn't have a multi-select option.

Does deleting a collection unsave the posts?

Usually not. A collection is a folder inside Saved, and most current guides say that deleting it removes only the folder: the posts stay in All posts. One older guide says the opposite, so check All posts afterwards. To delete a collection, open it, tap the three dots, choose Edit collection, then Delete collection. You can't delete All posts itself.

What about apps that unsave everything for you?

Browser extensions and apps that promise to clear your saves in one click exist, but they aren't made by Instagram. Some ask for your Instagram password or run inside your logged-in account, and fast bulk actions can get an account temporarily limited. If you use one, don't give it your password, and remove it when you're done. Doing it in batches in the app is slower but safer.

What should I do before I unsave everything?

Keep a copy. A save is only a bookmark, so once it's gone you'd have to find the reel again from scratch. Instagram will email you a file of every saved post with its link and caption: here's how to download your saved posts. Tick only Saved and choose HTML.

Then decide what's worth keeping. Drop the zip into SaveSort without unzipping it. It reads the saved-posts file in your browser, groups every save by topic and labels it Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked, so you can see which ones have real steps before you clear the rest. Nothing is uploaded.

Honest limits: SaveSort can't unsave anything for you. It only reads the file, and it judges by the caption. In our sample report, 30 of 156 saves were Do it and 10 were Skip it, so most piles have a short list worth keeping.
