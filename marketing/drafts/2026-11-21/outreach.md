## Community answer
For an "I can't find a reel I saved months ago" thread. Find a real thread first and read that community's rules; if self-links aren't allowed, post it without the last paragraph. (We couldn't load Reddit's r/Instagram rules page this week.)

> Saved has no search, sadly. Two things that work:
> 1. Profile → menu → Saved → All posts, then tap the reels icon so only reels show. Newest saves are at the top.
> 2. If it was months ago, ask Instagram for your saves: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download (or Export) your information → just Saved, Download to device, HTML. Open saved_posts.html from the zip and use Find in page with any word you remember from the caption. Each match links back to the reel.
> If it's gone completely, the creator probably deleted it or went private. Instagram doesn't tell you when that happens.
> Disclosure: I made a free tool, savesortai.com, that sorts that same zip by topic in your browser and labels them Do it / Watch it / Skip it / Locked (nothing uploaded). You don't need it; step 2 works on its own.
