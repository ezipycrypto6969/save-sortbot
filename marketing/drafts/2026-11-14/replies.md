1. **Is it safe?** You download your own data from Instagram and drop the zip on savesortai.com. It's read in your browser and nothing gets uploaded. We never ask for your Instagram login.
2. **Is it free?** Yes, sorting is free with no account and no limit. Pro (A$1.99 a week) is optional and writes a breakdown of a save. *(Owner: OK to mention Pro in replies? Last week's bank said not to.)*
3. **How do I get my file?** Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → Some of your information → tick only Saved → Download to device → All time, format HTML. Instagram emails you when it's ready, usually within a few hours. Drop the zip in as it is.
4. **What do the labels mean?** Do it: the steps are in the caption. Watch it: the caption says almost nothing, so the point is in the video. Skip it: hype, not help. Locked: "comment GUIDE" or "DM me" stands between you and the steps.
5. **HTML or JSON?** HTML. It includes the captions, which is what SaveSort reads. JSON has no captions, so everything would land in Watch it.
6. **Does it watch the videos?** No. It reads each caption. That's why near-empty captions get Watch it.
7. **TikTok or YouTube saves too?** Not yet. It only reads Instagram's Saved export.
8. **Is this Instagram/Meta?** No. SaveSort is independent and only uses the export Instagram gives you.
