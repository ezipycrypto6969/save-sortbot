## Community answer (for a "how do I clear my saved posts" thread)
Use only where the forum allows a disclosed self-mention; otherwise post it without the last paragraph. No Reddit thread was found by search, so find a real one first.

> Before you mass-unsave, download them. Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download (or Export) your information → just Saved, HTML. Instagram emails a zip, and then you've lost nothing.
> To unsave: Profile → menu → Saved → All posts. On some app versions you can tap ⋯ → Select to do several at once. If not, it's one at a time. Deleting a collection doesn't unsave what's in it.
> Disclosure: I made a free tool for the "which ones were worth keeping" part, savesortai.com. You drop the zip in, it sorts the saves by topic in your browser (nothing uploaded), and it flags the ones worth doing. Totally fine to skip it, the steps above work on their own.
