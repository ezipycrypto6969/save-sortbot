## New outline: `/blog/unsave-all-instagram-saved-posts`
- **Title (42 chars):** How to unsave all saved posts on Instagram
- **Meta description:** Clear out your Instagram Saved tab without losing the reels worth keeping: how unsaving works, what collections do, and how to keep a sorted copy first. Free.
- **Answer first (2 lines):** Instagram has no single "Unsave all" button. You unsave from Saved → All posts; some app versions let you select several at once. Download your saves first, so you keep a copy of the good ones.
- **H2 Can you unsave everything at once?** Sources disagree (Guiding Tech, Aug 2024: Saved → All posts → ⋯ → Select → Unsave; CodeItBro, Oct 2025: no built-in bulk option). The writer checks the current app and writes only what it sees.
- **H2 Before you clear it, download your saves.** Link to the download post: tick Saved, Download to device, HTML; Instagram emails a zip.
- **H2 How do you find the few worth keeping?** SaveSort sorts the zip by topic in your browser and labels saves Do it, Watch it, Skip it or Locked. Nothing uploaded, no login. Our sample report: 30 of 156 were Do it. Limits: reads captions, not videos. Link `/#try`.
