# Reply bank (drafts, owner sends). Never mention Pro until the owner confirms it is launched.

1. **Is it safe?** Good question to ask! You download your own data from Instagram, then drop the zip into savesortai.com. It's read in your browser, nothing gets uploaded, and we never ask for your Instagram login.
2. **Is it free?** Yep, sorting your saves is free. No account needed.
3. **How do I get my export?** Instagram > Settings > Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > Download your information > tick just Saved, format HTML, date range All time. Instagram emails you when it's ready (often within a few hours). Then drop the zip in as it is, no need to unzip.
4. **Does it work on iPhone?** It's a website, so there's no app to install. On iPhone, open savesortai.com in Safari, tap Choose file and pick the zip from Files. A laptop is easiest if your phone gives you any trouble. [OWNER: test once on an iPhone before using this reply]
5. **Can't Instagram already do this?** Partly! Saved has Collections, and Watch History (Your Activity) shows reels you watched. SaveSort just sorts all your saves by topic at once, and flags which are worth doing, which need the video and which to be careful with.
6. **Why HTML and not JSON?** HTML includes the captions, and that's what SaveSort reads. JSON only has links, so everything would land in "Needs the video".
7. **Is this an Instagram/Meta thing?** No. SaveSort is independent and not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. It only uses the export Instagram gives you.
8. **It put something in the wrong topic.** Thanks for telling us! The free sort goes by the words in each caption, so a gym reel that mentions "business" can end up in the wrong place. Which one was it? We'll use it to make it better.
9. **"Haha my saved tab is a disaster" (any friendly comment).** Same here, 156 and counting. Sorting them was the only way I found anything again.
