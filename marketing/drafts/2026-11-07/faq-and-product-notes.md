# FAQ answers and product notes (draft, from D10 Product marketing)

## FAQ (for comments, DMs and the site). Every answer matches what the free sorter does today.
- **Is it safe?** The free sort runs in your browser. You drop in the zip Instagram emails you; SaveSort opens only the saved-posts file and uploads nothing. No Instagram login, ever.
- **Is it free?** Yes. Every save sorted by topic and flagged worth doing / needs the video / be careful / gated guide. No account.
- **Too much effort?** You ask once: Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → tick only Saved, All time, format HTML. When the email comes, drop the zip in without unzipping it. The sort takes seconds.
- **iPhone?** Request the file in the Instagram app, download the zip from the email, then on savesortai.com tap "Choose file" and pick it from Files. [OWNER: test this once on iPhone Safari before using it.]
- **How long does Instagram take?** Instagram builds the file and emails you; the site says "usually within a few hours". Ticking only Saved keeps it small. Don't say "ten minutes".
- **HTML or JSON?** Pick HTML. JSON has no captions, so everything lands in "Needs the video".
- **Does it watch the videos?** No. It reads captions, which is why 65 of 156 were "needs the video".
- **Can't Instagram already do this?** Partly. Saved has Collections, and Watch history (Your activity) shows reels you watched. SaveSort sorts all your saves by topic at once and flags them.

## Product notes for the owner (#100). These come from marketing spotting gaps, not from users: we have 0 comments and no user questions yet. Please forward any DMs or emails.
1. **Site numbers don't match the reels** (312/21 in the hero, 156/24/61/7 in the strip vs 156/30/65/10/22). See `site-copy.md`.
2. **Pro isn't launched, but the page promises it** ("step-by-step plans", "fresh report every month", "search", a live checkout).
3. **No food/recipe, travel or gift topic.** Recipe and gift saves land in "Everything else". "Workout" alone isn't matched either (only "gym" and "fitness" are). "Worth doing" is keyed to AI words. The general public, and the planned kitchen and gym accounts, need these topics. A "Food & recipes" topic, plus `workout|exercise|reps` added to Health & lifestyle, would let the "find that recipe/workout" reels show the real thing.
4. **Phone flow:** no iPhone instructions, and drag-and-drop doesn't exist on phones.
5. **We can't see clicks or sign-ups.** Per-platform bio links (`?ref=ig`, `?ref=yt`, `?ref=tt`) and cookieless analytics would let us judge the link ask. Check it against the "nothing uploaded" privacy wording first.
