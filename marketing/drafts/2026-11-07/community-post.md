A few ways, quickest first:
1. Profile > menu (three lines) > Saved > All posts. It's newest first, so if it was recent just scroll.
2. If you put it in a Collection, it's in there under Saved.
3. If you watched it (not only saved it): Settings > Your activity > Watch history. You can filter by date or by account, handy if you remember roughly when or who.
4. Check Liked too, people often like and save at the same time.

If you have hundreds of saves and remember only the topic: disclosure, I made a free tool for this, savesortai.com. You download your Saved data from Instagram and it sorts every save by topic in your browser (nothing uploaded). Happy to help either way.
