Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 27 October 2026

Free trial

Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time before it ends and you won't be charged anything.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from the "manage or cancel" link on our pricing section. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

If you're charged and you're not happy, email us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it in full, no questions asked. After that, we refund where the law requires it, for example if Pro has a major problem we can't fix.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law and other consumer laws that apply to you aren't affected by this policy.

How to ask

Email eddieleafe3@gmail.com with the email address you used to pay. Refunds go back to the original card through Stripe and usually show within 5–10 business days.
