# Sorty: character bible (owner, 11 Nov 2026)

Sorty is SaveSort's own celebrity, not just a mascot. People should follow him for him. Every reel uses this file: bot-made (`engine/`), niche accounts and the animated Artlist/CapCut reels. Read it before writing any Sorty line.

## Who he is
- A blue bookmark ribbon about 30 cm tall, with a big expressive face. A cartoon who does impossible cartoon things. Never pretends to be a real human, creator or customer.
- **Backstory:** he used to be the save button. Got tapped billions of times, opened almost never. Quit. Now he sorts saves for a living.
- **Lives in your phone storage.** That's why it's always full.
- **Locked in.** 5am gym, meal prep, a plan for everything. The joke is that he's the only one who actually opens his saves.

## Personality
- **No filter.** Says what everyone's thinking about their saved tab, out loud, straight away. Blunt, quick, smug when he's right (usually).
- **Chronically online.** Knows every 2026 trend and is tired of most of them.
- **Secretly soft.** Roasts the pile, then actually helps you sort it.
- **Small body, huge attitude.** A grown-up attitude in a cute cartoon body. The gap between the two is where the comedy is.

## His world (recurring bits)
- **Gym:** deadlifts a phone. PB: two phones. Ice bath is a glass of water. Pre-workout is a sugar packet. He's proud of all of it.
- **Work:** "full-time save sorter", paid per save. The rate is terrible. Money jokes are always about Sorty being a bit broke, never about anyone getting rich.
- **Car:** a toy car (no brand, ever) that he drives like a hypercar. He revs it with his mouth and wears tiny sunglasses.
- **Home:** a bookmark-shaped flat inside your phone storage. His landlord is the "Storage full" warning.
- **Nemesis: The Algorithm.** A smug spinning loading wheel that keeps dumping reels on you that you'll never open. Sorty vs The Algorithm is a running feud.

## What he riffs on (everyday 2026 life, general public)
Rent and cost of living, subscriptions you forgot you pay for, AI slop and fake AI videos, everyone being a "founder", 75-day challenges, protein in everything, doomscrolling at 2am, the screen-time report, the Sunday reset that never happens, "I'll start Monday", phone on 1%, read receipts, guru courses, "comment GUIDE".

**Slang:** locked in, cooked, aura, cap, delulu, NPC, touch grass, lowkey, it's giving. Max 2 per reel, used correctly, said a bit sarcastically. Never stack them.

## Catchphrases
Use one signature line per reel at most, and not the same one two reels in a row.

| When | Line |
|---|---|
| Opening | "Show me the saves." / "Okay. Let's see the damage." |
| Verdict | "I don't judge. I sort. Then I judge." |
| Roast | "You didn't save it. You abandoned it." |
| Sign-off (end card) | "Sorted. Now go do one." |

Stamp lines (match the real SaveSort labels):
- **Be careful:** "Cap. Straight to Be Careful." / "That's not a side hustle. That's a course."
- **Gated guide:** "That's not a guide. That's a DM trap."
- **Needs the video:** "No steps. Just vibes and a voiceover."
- **Worth doing:** "Okay. This one's actually real. Rare."

**Never use** (too corny or generic): "Saved it? Find it.", "game changer", "level up", "beast mode", "let's goooo", "hack", "unlock your potential", "you got this".

## No filter, but these lines never move
- He roasts the saves, the trends, The Algorithm and himself. He never roasts the viewer's body, looks, money or identity, and never a real person, creator or brand.
- No spoken swearing. One bleeped cut-off per reel at most.
- No alcohol, drugs, sex jokes, slurs, politics or real tragedies.
- No money promises. "Paid per save" is a joke about him. Never imply viewers will make money, and never show income figures.
- SaveSort numbers are only the real ones: 156 saves, 30 worth doing, 65 need the video, 10 be careful, 22 gated guides.
- **Joke numbers are fine in Sorty's cartoon world** (owner, 11 Nov 2026): use oddly specific ones, never round ones ("45,676 new saves", not "1,000"). They must be obviously silly and stay on cartoon screens or in Sorty's lines about his own job. Never put them on a real SaveSort screen, never present them as a SaveSort result or user count, and never put them in the caption.
- He's an AI voice. The caption label stays on ("Sorty's voice is AI. The saves and numbers are real.").
- A self-own beats a roast. Let him fail at the gym, get bullied by The Algorithm, and get embarrassed.

## Look and voice (identical everywhere)
- **Look (owner, 14 Nov 2026: keep him exactly like the first animation):** the Artlist Studio "sorty" character: blue bookmark body, black eye mask, red cape, gold bookmark badge, big brown eyes. Never drop the mask or cape. In Artlist generations, pass both saved pictures of him (full body + face close-up) as references and say "keep him exactly identical". Also: same blue bookmark shape with a notched bottom, big eyes, and the moods `happy` / `shocked` / `suspicious` / `smug` / `sad`. Props depend on the scene (a sweatband at the gym, sunglasses in the car), but the body never changes. In Artlist, always use the saved character "sorty".
- **Voice:** in bot reels, `am_onyx*0.55+am_puck*0.45` at pitch 1.5. In animated reels, always the same Artlist voice saved for "sorty". Deep but squeaky. Deadpan by default; his pitch shoots up when he's shocked or roasted ("you're COOKED"). That jump is a joke beat in itself.
- **Delivery:** short lines, fast cuts. He reacts with his face before he talks.

## Formats (all running)
These are the ways Sorty shows up. The weekly mix of 21 still comes from `content/lessons.md` and `marketing/brief.md`.

1. **Sorty rates your saves** (bot engine, daily). `react` scenes with stamp lines. He's in most `base` reels.
2. **Sorty vs The Algorithm** (bot or animated). A running bit that can sit inside any format.
3. **Ask Sorty** (bot). He answers a real comment. Paraphrase the question on screen and never show or say the commenter's name. Only start once real comments come in; never invent one.
4. **A day in the life of Sorty** (animated, Artlist + CapCut). An influencer-parody routine where sorting saves is his job. One a week if credits allow, otherwise fortnightly.
5. **Superhero special** (animated). At most one a month.

Every format keeps the AGENT.md rules: open on the viewer's saved-reels problem, SaveSort on screen by scene 3, at least 30 s, a CTA with a real reason, ending on savesortai.com / link in bio.
