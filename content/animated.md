# Animated Sorty reels: scriptwriter rules (owner, 11 Nov 2026)

These rules are for the Pixar-style reels made in Artlist Studio and edited in CapCut. They come from what made the Trenchies-style animated reels work (fast, loud, a lot of movement). Sorty's personality and limits are in `content/sorty.md`. Every AGENT.md rule still applies.

## The 13 rules
1. **Hook in the first second.** A line about the viewer's saved reels ("Got 500 saved reels?"), with a sound, on frame one.
2. **A new picture every 1.5–2 seconds.** Aim for 18–22 cuts in 35 seconds. Get extra cuts for free with CapCut punch-ins, crops and re-used frames, not with more paid clips.
3. **Every shot moves.** Push-in, crash zoom, whip pan, shake, tilt or slow pan. No still picture on screen for more than 2 seconds.
4. **Every action has a sound effect.** The script has its own SFX column. Use our own effects (`engine/music.py`) or CapCut effects cleared for commercial use.
5. **Voice pitch swings.** Sorty is deadpan by default, with at least 3 jumps per reel: a squeak, a scream ("you're COOKED") and a shocked high line. One or two screams at most.
6. **Slang:** max 2 words per reel, used correctly (cooked, cap, locked in).
7. **Scribbly cartoon phone for the jokes, real proof once.** Jokes can use a cartoon phone and oddly specific joke numbers ("45,676 new saves"). One real SaveSort screen (about 2 s) appears within the first 5 seconds, showing only real numbers and the current label names.
8. **Music:** only original or properly licensed music (for example made by the owner in Artlist AI Music). Never a sound taken from someone else's reel. Line the biggest jokes up with the track's hits, and turn the music down under Sorty's lines.
9. **Same outro every time:** Sorty pushes the glowing phone at the camera, "Sorted. Now go do one.", savesortai.com / link in bio. It lands on the last music hit.
10. **Length:** 30–40 seconds.
11. **Script format:** a table with Time | Picture | Camera | Sorty says (and how) | SFX | Music | Make it with. Mark the music hits.
12. **Cost cap:** at most 3 new Artlist video clips per episode (about 2,500 credits each). Everything else is still frames (about 130 credits) or free CapCut edits. Show the owner the frames before making any clips.
13. **Never copy Trenchies itself:** no slogans, sounds or characters. No real people, brands or logos. A real creator can appear only in a real collab where they film their own part, and only if the owner changes the no-real-people rule first.
