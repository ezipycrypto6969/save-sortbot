# Animated Sorty reels: scriptwriter rules (owner, 11 Nov 2026)

These rules are for the Pixar-style reels made in Artlist Studio and edited in CapCut. They come from what made the Trenchies-style animated reels work (fast, loud, a lot of movement). Sorty's personality and limits are in `content/sorty.md`. Every AGENT.md rule still applies.

**How episodes are made:** script as JSON plus pictures, rendered in code by `episodes/` (Remotion). See `episodes/README.md`. CapCut is no longer needed.

## The 13 rules
1. **Hook in the first second.** A line about the viewer's saved reels ("Got 500 saved reels?"), with a sound, on frame one.
2. **A new picture every 1.5–2 seconds.** Aim for 18–22 cuts in 35 seconds. Get extra cuts for free with CapCut punch-ins, crops and re-used frames, not with more paid clips.
3. **Every shot moves.** Push-in, crash zoom, whip pan, shake, tilt or slow pan. No still picture on screen for more than 2 seconds.
4. **Every action has a sound effect.** The script has its own SFX column. Use our own effects (`engine/music.py`) or CapCut effects cleared for commercial use.
5. **Voice pitch swings.** Sorty is deadpan by default, with at least 3 jumps per reel: a squeak, a scream ("you're COOKED") and a shocked high line. One or two screams at most.
6. **Slang:** max 2 words per reel, used correctly (cooked, cap, locked in).
7. **Scribbly cartoon phone for the jokes, real proof once.** Jokes can use a cartoon phone and oddly specific joke numbers ("45,676 new saves"). One real SaveSort screen (about 2 s) appears within the first 5 seconds, showing only real numbers and the current label names.
8. **Music:** only original or properly licensed music (for example made by the owner in Artlist AI Music). Never a sound taken from someone else's reel. Line the biggest jokes up with the track's hits, and turn the music down under Sorty's lines.
9. **Same outro every time:** Sorty pushes the glowing phone at the camera, "Sorted. Now go do one.", savesortai.com / link in bio. It lands on the last music hit.
10. **Length:** 30–40 seconds.
11. **Script format:** a table with Time | Picture | Camera | Sorty says (and how) | SFX | Music | Make it with. Mark the music hits.
12. **Cost cap (owner approved 14 Nov 2026):** up to 8 moving clips per episode, made with Kling 3 Pro 1080p image-to-video (5 s, no audio, about 500 credits each, so about 4,000 credits an episode). Start frames are Sorty stills (about 90 credits each, reuse them). Never hold a still picture on screen. Always quote the price and get the owner's OK before generating.
13. **Never copy Trenchies itself:** no slogans, sounds or characters. No real people, brands or logos. A real creator can appear only in a real collab where they film their own part, and only if the owner changes the no-real-people rule first.

## Getting people to interact (owner, 11 Nov 2026)
People only comment, send, save or follow when they get something out of it. Every reel gives them one clear reason. These add to the "Give the ask a reason" rules in AGENT.md (no pin, flex or bragging asks).

14. **One ask per reel, with a real reward.** Pick the one action that fits the reel and say what the viewer gets for doing it.
15. **Sends beat everything on Instagram.** Build in a "that's so them" moment (the mate with 2,000 gym saves, the partner with 40 recipe reels) and name who to send it to: "Send this to the mate whose saved tab is 4 years of leg days."
16. **Comments need a payoff.** Ask for something Sorty will actually answer or use. Examples: "Comment your most useless save. Sorty stamps the best 3 in Friday's reply video." / "Comment the topic drowning your saves. The top answer picks Sorty's next episode." Only promise replies and episodes we will really make (the Ask Sorty format).
17. **Make them play along inside the reel.** Examples: "Spot the fake: one of these 3 saves is hype. Pick before Sorty stamps it." / "Count the protein reels that fly past. Answer at the end." / "Which one does Sorty skip? Lock in your pick." Pay it off later in the same reel. This gets comments and rewatches without begging.
18. **Open a loop in the first 2 seconds and close it at the end.** The reveal is the reward for watching to the end.
19. **Running jokes make fans.** Bring back The Algorithm, the phone deadlift and the toy car so regulars recognise them and comment about them ("the Algorithm again").
20. **Follows need a promise that's real.** "Episode 2 Friday: Sorty vs your recipe saves." Only if episode 2 is already made or queued.
21. **Saves are for useful bits.** "Save this for when you export your saves" works on how-tos, not on comedy.
22. **Reply fast.** Answer comments in the first hour after posting, and pin one comment that asks a real question.
23. **Never use engagement bait.** No "comment YES", "like if…", "tag 10 friends", fake giveaways or fake drama. Meta and TikTok push these down, and they break our honesty rules.

## From the 20-reel study (owner, 14 Nov 2026; details in `research/animation-study-2026-11.md`)
24. **It's a series.** One character, one voice, every shot. Every episode gets a title card ("Ep 1: The 4,000 Saves") and the same ElevenLabs voice. Never switch voices between episodes.
25. **Story Loop, not a montage.** Stakes in second one → a question the viewer wants answered → the twist → a rehook (next episode or "Part 1"). The product is the twist that solves the problem, never an interruption.
26. **Everyday "that's so them" stories beat clever ones.** Gym saves, recipe saves, a mate, a boss, a partner. Write for the send.
27. **Comment keyword only with a real auto-DM.** "Comment SORT and Sorty DMs you the link" is allowed only once the auto-DM really sends the link. Until then the CTA is savesortai.com / link in bio. This is the biggest lever for comments and sends we found.
28. **Light it like the winners.** Pixar-style soft key light, warm character, cooler blue background, soft contact shadow, shallow focus, subject centred. Problem scenes are darker and cooler; the win is brighter and warmer.
29. **Rhythm:** a calm 2–4 s setup, then 0.7–1.5 s shots, then a 0.2–0.5 s montage on the win. Captions are one word at a time, centred, outlined, with one highlight colour.
30. **End on something real:** the last 2–3 s before the outro show the real SaveSort report with real numbers.
