## Experiment ideas (general public, marketing-focused, no fake people or testimonials)

- `exp-pov`: relatable POV ("POV: you open your saved tab looking for one recipe").
- `exp-before-after`: the messy saved tab, then the sorted SaveSort list.
- `exp-myth-fact`: one viral claim from a saved reel, then what's actually true.
- `exp-challenge`: "I did one saved reel a day for a week" style, only with real, checkable results.
- `exp-signs`: "3 signs your saved tab is a junk drawer".
- `exp-question`: asks viewers one question to comment ("How many saves do you have?").
- `exp-stitch`: reacts to a common type of reel (paraphrased, no real creator named).
