# SaveSort Gym (playbook)

**Who:** everyday gym-goers and people trying to start: the ones with 200 saved workouts and the same 3 machines every session. Not bodybuilders, not influencers.
**Promise:** "The workouts you saved, finally done." Simple, safe routines you'll actually follow, and a way to find the ones you saved.
**Voice:** Sorty as the slightly unfit gym buddy. Dry, encouraging, never body-shaming. No "beast mode" bro talk.
**Look:** near-black with lime accent (`accounts.json`).

## Slideshow formats (rotate)

| Style | What it is | Example hook |
|---|---|---|
| `story` | Stronger-style tension → release. A relatable gym moment, the fix, then SaveSort found the routine in your saves. | "Skipped legs *again*?" |
| `routine` | A full, simple routine (4–5 moves, sets × reps) people save. Last slide: "you'll save this and lose it — here's how not to". | "20 minute push day. *No queue.*" |
| `mistakes` | 3–5 common form or plan mistakes, with the fix. | "You're wasting your rest days" |
| `saved-vs-did` | What people save vs what they actually do (honest, funny). | "Saved: 47 ab workouts. Did: 0." |
| `beginner` | First week / first month plans for nervous starters. | "Scared of the gym? *Start here.*" |
| `myth` | A common gym myth from saved reels and what's actually true (sourced). | "Your saved reel says: no carbs after 6" |

## Reel formats (19:30)

Sorty reacts to a viral gym claim (verdict stamp), or "I found every leg workout I ever saved" with real SaveSort screens (Health & lifestyle topic).

## Topics to rotate

legs, push/pull, home workouts, no-equipment, beginners, consistency, rest and sleep habits, gym anxiety, walking, stretching.

## Must-follow

No calorie/macro targets, weight-loss promises, supplements or medical advice. Always a safety note on harder moves ("light weight first; stop if it hurts"). Real exercise names only.
