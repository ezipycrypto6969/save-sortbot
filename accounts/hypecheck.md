# SaveSort Hype Check (playbook)

**Who:** people whose feeds are full of "make $10k a month with AI" and side-hustle reels, who half-believe them. The general public, not tech people.
**Promise:** "We check the reels you saved, so you don't waste your weekend." Honest verdicts: worth doing, needs more info, or be careful. This is SaveSort's "be careful" flag as a whole account.
**Voice:** Sorty as the deadpan fact-checker. Calm, fair, a bit Aussie ("Mate. No."). Explains why; never mocks a person.
**Look:** ink with warning-orange and yellow (`accounts.json`).

## Slideshow formats (rotate)

| Style | What it is | Example hook |
|---|---|---|
| `verdict` | One viral claim (paraphrased, no creator named) → what's actually true → verdict stamp → SaveSort flags these in your saves. | "\"$500 a day with AI art\"" |
| `red-flags` | 4–5 signs a side-hustle reel is hype. | "5 signs that reel is selling *you*" |
| `real-cost` | What a "free" or "easy" hustle actually needs: time, tools, real prices (checked, USD). | "The 'free' hustle that costs $300" |
| `gated` | "Comment GUIDE" bait: what you actually get when you comment. | "What 'comment GUIDE' really sends you" |
| `worth-it` | The rare ones that hold up, honestly framed (effort, skills, no income promises). | "3 saved hustles that *aren't* hype" |
| `story` | Tension → release: you saved a get-rich reel at 2am → what happened → SaveSort's be-careful pile. | "I saved it at 2am." |

## Reel formats (19:30)

Sorty `react` scenes rating saved-reel claims with verdict stamps, plus real SaveSort screens of the Be careful filter.

## Must-follow

Every fact checked against a primary source (the claims checker will hold anything unverified). Never name or show a real creator, never copy their words beyond a short paraphrase. No income claims of our own, no financial or legal advice ("check the rules where you live"). Global audience: no country-specific laws or agencies.
