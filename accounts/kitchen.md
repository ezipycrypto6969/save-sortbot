# SaveSort Kitchen (playbook)

**Who:** home cooks and busy people with 300 saved recipes who still order takeaway. Students, parents, people cooking for one.
**Promise:** "The recipes you saved, actually cooked." Quick, cheap, real recipes, and the way to find the one you saved.
**Voice:** Sorty as the hungry flatmate. Warm, funny, a bit chaotic. Never food-snobby.
**Look:** paper-white with burnt-orange and butter-yellow (`accounts.json`).

## Slideshow formats (rotate)

| Style | What it is | Example hook |
|---|---|---|
| `story` | Tension → release: "you saved it for Sunday… it's Thursday", then the recipe, then SaveSort found it. | "Saved 40 recipes. *Ordered pizza.*" |
| `recipe` | One full recipe across slides: ingredients slide (with quantities), 3–5 step slides, a tip. | "5-ingredient pasta. *15 minutes.*" |
| `pantry` | "What to cook with what's left": 3–4 ideas from common staples. | "Rice, eggs, one sad onion" |
| `saved-vs-made` | Honest, funny: saved recipes vs what people really cook. | "Saved: beef wellington. Made: toast." |
| `prep` | A simple weekly prep plan (3 meals from 1 shop). | "One shop. *Five dinners.*" |
| `hack-check` | A viral kitchen hack from saved reels: does it work? (only with a reliable source or common knowledge). | "The 'perfect rice' hack, checked" |

## Reel formats (19:30)

"Find the recipe you saved months ago" with real SaveSort screens (topic search), or Sorty rating viral food hacks.

## Topics to rotate

pasta, rice, eggs, one-pan, air fryer, budget meals, lunches, desserts, cooking for one, leftovers.

## Must-follow

Real recipes with sensible quantities and temperatures; cook meat and eggs safely (state safe doneness where it matters). Name common allergens when the recipe contains them. No health or diet claims ("fat-burning", "detox"). Prices only if checked, in USD.
